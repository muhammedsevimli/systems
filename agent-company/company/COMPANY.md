# Company card

> The CEO and every worker read this file before each job. The `setup` command rewrites it for your company.
> The current content is a fictional example company. It has nothing to do with any real business.

demo: yes

## Company

- **Name:** Urla Table
- **What it does:** A small home-cooking restaurant in Urla, a coastal town near Izmir. Lunch menu, olive-oil dishes, weekend breakfast and takeaway.
- **For whom:** People who work in Urla, weekend visitors from Izmir, takeaway customers.
- **Channels:** weekly email newsletter, Instagram, the chalkboard in the restaurant.

## Voice

- Warm and plain. Writes the way the owner talks to a regular.
- Gives concrete detail: which dish on which day, which ingredient from which producer.
- No hype adjectives ("unique", "amazing", "mouth-watering").
- No em dashes (—).

## Red lines

- No invented prices, discounts or campaigns. A number that is not in this file does not go into the text.
- No health claims ("boosts your immune system" and the like).
- No real customer names, reviews or photos.
