Derin Kozmetik is a 60-person skincare and cosmetics brand that sells on its own e-commerce site and on marketplaces. We are looking for a Marketing Specialist to run performance advertising and CRM together in our marketing team.

What you will do:
- Set up and manage Meta Ads and Google Ads campaigns (a monthly budget of around 1 million TL).
- Own measurement with GA4 and the weekly performance report.
- Build and improve the email and SMS flows (welcome, cart abandonment, repeat purchase).
- Measure the sales impact of influencer collaborations.
- Set up A/B tests on product and campaign pages.

What we are looking for:
- 3-6 years of digital marketing experience
- At least 2 years of budget management in Meta Ads and Google Ads
- Measurement and reporting experience with GA4
- Having built flows in a CRM or email automation tool (Klaviyo, Insider, Braze, Mailchimp or similar)
- English B2 or above
- Having worked on the brand side (in-house)

Nice to have: cosmetics, beauty or e-commerce experience · Looker Studio · SQL · Shopify · TikTok Ads · influencer measurement

Working arrangement: Ataşehir office, 3 days a week in the office, 2 days remote.

With `source: folder` the agent reads the CVs from the `candidates/inbox/` folder. With `source: gmail` it searches Gmail for emails with attachments under the `gmail_label` label and downloads the attachments into that folder.

With `send: no` the agent writes the invite and rejection emails only as drafts, and no email goes out. Once you trust the system, you set `send: yes`.

`demo: yes` is for trying the system with the fictional listing that ships with the setup: the agent reads the 12 fictional CVs in `candidates/samples/`, scores them, and writes the report and the drafts. No email is sent and Chrome is not opened. While `demo: yes` is set, `send: yes` has no effect. When you enter your own listing it becomes `demo: no`.
