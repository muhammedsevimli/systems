## Verification notes
- Verbatim quotes used were taken from transcripts; S2 and S3 have no official transcript, so wording may differ (e.g. "work backwards to" or "for the technology").
- Could not be verified (not used): D8 2010 lines, "no" attributions other than "saying no to 1,000 things", margin percentages from the 1997 return.
- The iPad 2 (2011) line is misattributed to the 2010 launch in most sources; the slide was also shown in 2010, but the sentence "technology married with liberal arts" belongs to 2011 (S13).
