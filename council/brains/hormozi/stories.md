# Hormozi: stories

Most of the stories rest on Hormozi's own telling and are compiled from secondary sources. Claims about revenue, numbers and dates should be read as "marketing_claim" and should not be made the basis of a decision.

1. **The closed gyms.** Hormozi says he left a consulting job at 23 and opened a gym, started with five gyms, closed the sixth and "lost everything"; then he went back to opening gyms and lost a second time (his own video description, S14, secondary). Principle: scale without profit eats cash; choosing the wrong constraint is costly.

2. **Knowledge turned into a product.** He is said to have sold the marketing knowledge he gained from opening gyms to gym owners; Gym Launch was founded around 2016, and the dates differ across sources (S14, S17, secondary). Principle: see the demand first, then package the product. Because there is a buyer, the question "will people pay for this?" is answered.

3. **Gym Launch Secrets.** The book is offered through an Audible trial or postal delivery, and there is no free PDF (S12). Principle: the book works as a low-friction lead magnet that builds trust before selling the service.

4. **The Win Your Money Back logic.** In Money Models, a win-your-money-back setup appears as an example of an attraction entry offer; it is said that this kind of "challenge" package is used in gyms (summary, S3). The details need verification. Principle: in the entry offer, the risk shows up on you, not on the customer, but the price of that is the first 30 days of profit.

5. **The Money Models launch.** In August 2025 the book was announced with a live stream, and four special bonuses were added to the pre-order (S3, secondary press). Principle: a real, dated window and a bonus create urgency without fake scarcity. Copy counts have not been independently verified; they are not told.

6. **The sale claim.** It is written that the majority stake in Gym Launch and Prestige Labs was sold in 2021 for about 46 million dollars (S17, secondary, not verified). Principle: measure a revenue claim for your own decision; don't lean on someone else's marketing_claim.

7. **Acquisition.com.** It is said to have been founded in 2020 as a "family office," meaning it invests its own money in businesses (S14, S17 secondary). Principle: when it is his own money, profit discipline becomes firm.
