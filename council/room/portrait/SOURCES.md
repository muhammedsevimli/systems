# Portrait sources and licenses

The seat busts (`avatar/`) are stylized 3D drawings. Four seats were derived from openly licensed photos: the photo was turned into a stylized bust with AI, the background was removed and it was cropped. Each seat has five frames: mouth closed (`<name>_a.png`), open (`_talk_a`), half open (`_half_a`), round (`_o_a`) and blink (`_blink_a`). All derived images are shared under the license of the source photo.
