# Should I open a second branch for Urla Sofrası?

## Decision
Which growth path should Urla Sofrası choose to raise monthly net profit from 85,000 TL to at least 130,000 TL within 18 months?

## Options
- **A:** A second branch in Çeşme, in the summer-holiday area. Estimated setup 1,800,000 TL, half of it (900,000 TL) financed by a loan. 5-year lease, high deposit.
- **B:** Instead of a second branch, rent the shop next to the current location and grow capacity from 40 to 65 seats. Setup 650,000 TL, 2-year lease.
- **C:** Without opening a branch, set up a small production kitchen to grow takeout and ready-meal (jarred, frozen) sales. Setup 300,000 TL.
- **D:** Do nothing, stay with the current setup for one more year.

## Context
- A home-cooking restaurant in central Urla, 3 years old, 40 seats, open only at lunch and early evening.
- Run by the owner and their spouse, with 4 employees.
- Average monthly revenue 620,000 TL, net profit about 85,000 TL per month (net margin about 13.7%).
- Full at weekday lunch, a queue forms on weekends.
- Takeout is 18% of revenue (about 112,000 TL per month).

## Reversibility
- **A:** Close to a one-way door. 5-year lease, high deposit, 900,000 TL loan.
- **B:** Half two-way. 2-year contract, renovation cost becomes a sunk cost.
- **C:** Close to a two-way door. The equipment can be sold second-hand.
- **D:** Fully reversible, but with a one-year opportunity cost.

## Success criterion
Monthly net profit of at least 130,000 TL within 18 months (+45,000 TL from today), WITHOUT an increase in the owner's weekly working hours.

## Chair's assumptions (not given by the user, reasonable estimates)
1. Loan interest is about 45% per year (SME commercial loan); for 900,000 TL that is a monthly interest burden of about 34,000 TL.
2. The peak season in the Çeşme summer area is about 4 months (June-September); in winter, revenue drops to one third of summer revenue.
3. The owner's current work week is about 60 hours; the spouse works a similar amount. There is no spare hours capacity.
4. Customers lost in the weekend queue were not counted by observation; the estimate is that 20-30% of weekend meal demand turns away.
5. Food cost is about 35% of revenue, staff 25%, rent and fixed costs about 15%.
6. The food production permit and label requirements for ready meals (jarred, frozen) take 2-3 months.
7. During the renovation of the next-door shop, the main dining room can operate only partially for at most 2 weeks.

## The user's leaning
Kept with the Chair, not shown to the seats.
