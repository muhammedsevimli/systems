# Sealed opinion (Chair)

This opinion was written before the council was called. It does not enter the round prompts and is not changed afterward.

## My recommendation
**B** (next-door shop, 65 seats), supported by a small version of C. But a 4-week queue count is required before B.

## Three reasons
1. **The demand is already proven and on site.** Weekday lunch is full and there is a queue on weekends. B captures the part of the existing customer base that is being lost; Çeşme has to build brand-new demand from zero.
2. **The numbers hold, and no debt is needed.** 25 extra seats at about 50% average occupancy bring roughly 150,000-190,000 TL of extra monthly revenue; with a 25-30% contribution margin that means 40,000-55,000 TL of extra net profit. The 650,000 TL pays back in about 13-16 months. In Çeşme, the monthly interest on the 900,000 TL loan alone eats most of the target (+45,000 TL).
3. **The owner's hours.** B grows under the same roof, with the same team and the same kitchen; by hiring two more people the owner's hours can be held constant. Çeşme needs a second manager or splits the owner in two over the summer, which directly violates the success criterion.

## Biggest risk
The extra 25 seats fill only on weekends and weekday lunch and stay empty in the early evening. Then the extra revenue drops to half of the estimate and the target is not met in 18 months. Also, if the dining room is enlarged before the kitchen capacity reaches 65 seats, service quality drops.

## About Çeşme
A one-way door, seasonal revenue, debt financing and the risk of raising the owner's hours, all together. It is the only option that threatens both conditions of the success criterion (profit and hours) at the same time.

## Confidence
Medium. The real size of the demand lost in the queue is unknown; that number either strengthens B or moves C ahead.
