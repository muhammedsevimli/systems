## Strongest reason

B grows this experience, it does not invent something new. Çeşme means a different season, a different customer and a table managed from far away. If the kitchen and service cannot carry 65 seats, the taste of the table is spoiled. Speed breaks first, then flavor. You and your spouse already work 60 hours. If the dining room grows and your hours grow, you lose the success criterion.
