## Unanimity warning
In position, four seats are on B, but the real unanimity is on A: all five seats gave A a 2 or a 3, and all five called the ruin risk for A "high". No seat defended A. This is not a victory, it is a warning. The council never discussed A in its strongest form; it only rejected the "5-year lease plus loan" version.

**Mandatory discussion item (round 2):** What is the strongest defense of A? Is there a reversible version of the Çeşme idea, without the 5-year lease and the loan? If there is, under what condition does that version beat B?

## Shared blind spot
All five seats asked the same question: how many people turn back in the weekend queue? The council's whole B thesis rests on an unmeasured number. This number also determines the choice between B and C.

## Sharpest disagreement
**Between Bezos and Jobs.** Bezos keeps C in front (two-way door, the takeout channel is already 18%). Jobs puts C on the "no list" (a second product, a permit, a new customer, loss of focus). One sees C as a cheap experiment, the other as a side road that scatters the brand.

## The point no seat raised
The owner's hours. When B grows to 65 seats, how many new employees are needed, and how much of the +45,000 TL target does this staff cost take back? The seats mentioned the hours risk, but nobody put a number on it. This item is directed to Buffett and Hormozi in round 2.

## Round 2 conflict question
**"Is it more right to enter the 650,000 TL B without counting the queue, or to first enter through the two-way door with the 300,000 TL C and collect the data? And if there is a version of A that can be tried without the lease and the loan, does that version change this ranking?"**

Additional tasks:
- Musk: describe the fastest and cheapest testable version of A, or say why there is none.
- Buffett and Hormozi: include the extra staff cost in B; does the net contribution reach +45,000 TL?
